The story behind the journey

Not the end of my story.

My name is Steven. After decades of struggling with alcohol, I'm learning that a new beginning is possible. Recovery isn't a straight road. It's a daily decision to keep moving toward a life grounded in faith, honesty, and hope.

I created My Recovery Journey to share what I'm learning, connect with others, and remind anyone who feels lost that they don't have to walk alone.
