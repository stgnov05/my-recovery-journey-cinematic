My Story

A personal account of recovery, faith, and choosing to begin again.

My story isn't finished.

My name is Steven. After 34 years of struggling with alcohol, I'm rebuilding my life one day at a time.

For years, addiction took away opportunities, stability, relationships, and much of the life I hoped to build. There were days when I felt lost and wondered whether I could find peace or purpose again.

Today, I am choosing recovery. I am learning to face my past honestly, strengthen my faith in God, and build a future through small, daily decisions. It isn't always easy, and I don't pretend to have all the answers.

I created this site to share what I'm learning and to remind others who feel alone that a different chapter is still possible.

The past is part of my story. It doesn't have to write my future.
